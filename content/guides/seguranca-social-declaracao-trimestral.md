Four times a year — by the end of **January, April, July and October** — you
declare what you invoiced in the previous quarter. Segurança Social uses it to
set the contribution you'll pay each month for the next three months.

## Who needs to do it

Everyone with open activity as a trabalhador independente, **except** during the
first 12 months after you first open activity (you're exempt from contributions
then, and Segurança Social tells you when it ends).

## Steps

1. Log in to [Segurança Social Direta](https://app.seg-social.pt).
2. Go to **Emprego → Trabalhadores independentes → Declaração trimestral**
   (the menu names move around occasionally — search for "declaração trimestral").
3. The income from your faturas-recibo is usually pre-filled. Check it, and add
   anything that wasn't invoiced through the Portal das Finanças.
4. Submit. You'll see the contribution base and the monthly amount.

## Then pay every month

Contributions are paid between the **10th and the 20th** of each month. Get the
payment reference on Segurança Social Direta, or set up a direct debit
(*débito direto*) so you never miss it.
