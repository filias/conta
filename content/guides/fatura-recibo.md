Every time you're paid for a service you issue a **fatura-recibo** on the Portal
das Finanças. It's free and takes a couple of minutes once you've done it once.

## Before you start

- Your activity must be open (*início de atividade*) with the right CAE/CIRS code.
- Have your client's **NIF** (or VAT number and country if they're abroad), the
  amount, and the date you were paid.

## Steps

1. Log in to the [Portal das Finanças](https://www.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt).
2. Search for **"Faturas e Recibos Verdes"** and choose **Emitir**.
3. Pick **Fatura-Recibo** (you've already been paid) or **Fatura** (you'll be
   paid later, then issue a **Recibo** when the money arrives).
4. Fill in the transmission date, client, and a description of the service.
5. Choose the VAT regime that applies to you — if you're exempt, select the
   **art. 53.º** exemption reason.
6. Choose the IRS withholding (*retenção na fonte*) — clients with organised
   accounting in Portugal usually have to withhold; private individuals and
   foreign clients don't. Below a yearly income threshold you can claim the
   *dispensa de retenção* (art. 101.º-B CIRS).
7. Check the preview, issue it, and send the PDF to your client.

## Tips

- You can't edit an issued document — cancel it (*anular*) and issue a new one.
- The amounts feed straight into your Segurança Social quarterly declaration and
  your IRS Anexo B, so getting them right now saves work later.
